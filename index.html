TypeSafe AI demo

Ticket deadline likelihood

Paste a JIRA ticket and choose a deadline. TypeSafe will estimate the likelihood one engineer can finish the ticket by that date in a single decision.

This is a model-estimated probability, not a calibrated guarantee. It assumes one engineer works full-time on the ticket, excludes today and weekends when counting available workdays, and does not account for holidays, other work, blockers, reviews, dependencies, or team scheduling.
